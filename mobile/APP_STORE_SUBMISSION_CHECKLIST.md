## Common Rejection Reasons

### iOS
1. **Incomplete information** - Ensure all fields filled
2. **Broken features** - Test thoroughly before submission
3. **Misleading content** - Ensure description matches functionality
4. **Privacy policy issues** - Must be accessible and accurate
5. **Demo account issues** - Provide working test credentials

### Android
1. **Policy violations** - Review Google Play policies
2. **Broken features** - Test on multiple devices
3. **Inappropriate content** - Ensure content rating accurate
4. **Privacy policy missing** - Must be accessible URL
5. **Permissions not justified** - Explain why each permission needed
